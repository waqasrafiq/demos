GOOD TO KNOW

Straight answers

الأسئلة الشائعة

Genuine vs OEM vs aftermarket?

Genuine parts come in the car maker's own box and carry its brand. OEM parts are made by the same factory that supplies the car maker, usually without the car maker's brand, at a lower price. Aftermarket parts are made by other manufacturers and vary in quality and price. We label every part clearly as genuine, OEM or aftermarket before you buy.

Do your parts come with a warranty?

Yes. New parts carry a warranty against manufacturing defects. The period depends on the part and brand and is written on your invoice. Electrical parts are checked before they leave the shop. Used parts are sold as inspected, with limited or no warranty, and we tell you which before you pay.

How do I find a part by VIN?

The VIN (chassis number) is a 17-character code on your Mulkiya (vehicle registration card), on the lower left of the windscreen and on the driver door pillar. Send it with the part name through the request form or WhatsApp. It identifies the exact model, engine and build date, so we match the right part number.

Where do you deliver, and how fast?

We deliver in Sharjah, Ajman and Dubai. Orders confirmed in the morning are normally delivered the same day. Other emirates are usually next day. Garages and workshops can set up a regular delivery run.

Can you export outside the UAE?

Yes. We export to GCC countries and to Africa by road, sea and air freight. We prepare the export packing list and invoice, and can consolidate several parts into one shipment.

Payment and credit terms?

We accept cash, card and bank transfer. Registered workshops and garages can apply for a trade account with credit terms after a short check. Ask on WhatsApp for terms and the bulk price list.
