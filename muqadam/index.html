Questions

أسئلة شائعة

Do you sell genuine Toyota & Lexus parts?

Yes, genuine parts are our core business. Every quote shows the exact part number you will receive.

Do you sell wholesale and export?

Yes. Most of our customers are traders, garages and exporters. We supply across the UAE and export to Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Yemen and Algeria.

What if my part number has been superseded?

We check every number against its supersession. If the manufacturer has replaced your number, we quote the current one and tell you.

What if I don't know the part number?

Send the chassis number (VIN), the car model and year, and a photo of the part. We find the correct number for you.
