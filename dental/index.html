Good to know

Questions, answered

الأسئلة الشائعة

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes. We accept most major UAE dental insurance plans with direct billing. Bring your insurance card and Emirates ID, and our team will confirm your coverage and co-payment before treatment starts.

Do you treat dental emergencies?

Yes. We see emergencies such as severe toothache, broken or knocked-out teeth and swelling on the same day, subject to availability. Call or WhatsApp us during opening hours and we will fit you in as quickly as possible.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We offer kids' dentistry from age 2, including gentle check-ups, fluoride, fissure sealants and first-visit familiarisation so children feel relaxed.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. For larger treatments such as implants and braces we offer instalment plans, and we accept cash, cards and contactless payment. Ask the team for a written estimate before you begin.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Professional whitening supervised by a licensed dentist is considered safe for most healthy adults. We check your teeth and gums first, use protective gel on the gums, and may advise against whitening if you have untreated decay or very sensitive teeth.

How much does a first visit cost?

A first visit with a check-up and consultation starts from AED 150. If X-rays or cleaning are needed, we tell you the price before we begin, so there are no surprises.
