— Good to know

Questions, answered

الأسئلة الشائعة

Is there parking near Velour Rose Ladies Salon?

Yes. Free street and shared parking is available on the streets around the salon in Al Nuaimiya, Ajman. Please allow a few extra minutes at peak evening times.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome for threading, nails and quick styling when a stylist is free. For hair colour, keratin, facials and bridal services we recommend booking on WhatsApp first.

Do you offer home service in Ajman?

Yes. Home service is available across Ajman for bridal makeup, henna, hair styling and nails by appointment, with a small travel fee depending on the area. Message us on WhatsApp with your location.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash in AED, Visa and Mastercard debit and credit cards, and Apple Pay. A deposit for bridal bookings can be paid by bank transfer.

How do I book a bridal package?

Message us on WhatsApp at least 3 weeks before your date. We arrange a free consultation and a trial look, then confirm your package with a 30% deposit. Bridal packages start from AED 1,800.
